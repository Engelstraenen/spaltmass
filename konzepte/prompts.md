## SPSB-1 „Streiflicht“ (dunkles Studio, Cyan-Messlicht)

SPSB-1. Website storyboard sheet: one single image, a 3x3 grid of nine 16:9 panels on a near-black background with thin gutters, a small white panel number (1-9) in the top-left corner of each panel. Every panel is a photorealistic, high-end screen state of the same award-winning website for a fictional German car damage assessor company called SPALTMASS (Kfz-Sachverständige). Cinematic studio lighting, real photography inside the web design, premium Awwwards-style layout, bold modern grotesk sans-serif typography in white, deep black and graphite color world with electric cyan as the only accent color. Short German headlines only, spelled exactly as given. Panel 1: full-screen hero, a dark graphite modern car in a black photo studio, front left fender and door visibly dented, one long horizontal LED light bar reflected across the body so the dent distorts the reflection line, small top navigation with the word SPALTMASS, white headline "Wir sehen jeden Schaden." Panel 2: same car, same angle, an automotive damage assessor in his thirties in a black polo shirt presses a handheld paint thickness gauge against the fender, glowing cyan measurement dots float on the car body with small labels "142 µm" and "386 µm", step label "1 Messen". Panel 3: transition, the same car turns into a precise glowing cyan wireframe line drawing on black, the damaged zones on fender and door glow in cyan with thin callout lines, headline "Jeder Millimeter dokumentiert." Panel 4: section "Ihr Recht", huge kinetic white typography on black "Ihr Gutachter. Ihre Wahl." with three slim cyan cards below "Anrufen", "Vor Ort in 24 h", "Gutachten digital". Panel 5: a hand holding a modern smartphone, the camera view shows a dented car bumper inside a cyan guide frame with the label "Schaden fotografieren" and an upload progress bar. Panel 6: a photorealistic printed assessment report floating in 3D, pages fanning out, damage photos with yellow scale rulers on the pages, cyan checkmarks, headline "Gutachten in 48 h". Panel 7: the same graphite car fully repaired in the same black studio, the LED reflection line now perfectly straight across fender and door, headline "Wie neu. Und belegt." Panel 8: careers section, a confident young woman in her early twenties in a black workwear jacket holding a tablet next to a car in a modern workshop, huge kinetic words "Kfz-Profi? Werde Gutachter." and a cyan button "In 60 Sekunden bewerben". Panel 9: reviews and contact section, a large rating number with five stars filling in cyan, a thin scrolling ticker band, the small label "Beispiel", and a big cyan button "Schaden melden". Ultra sharp, consistent design across all nine panels, no real brand logos, no prices, no euro signs.
