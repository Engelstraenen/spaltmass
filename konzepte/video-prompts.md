# SPALTMASS – Video-Prompts (Drafts, je nur das 4K-Startbild als einzige Referenz)

Endrender später nur MiniMax H3 2K (≤15 s) nach Freigabe. Drafts 10 s.

## V1 Hero „Streiflicht“ (Startbild 01-hero-streiflicht, Wan 3.0 Unlimited, 480P, 16:9, 10 s)

One single continuous 10-second shot, real-time, no cuts. Use the reference image as the exact first frame. Layout, left to right: dark empty studio space on the left, the dark graphite unbranded hatchback in the center-right, three-quarter front view from the front left, a long horizontal white LED light bar hanging on thin cables above the car. The car never moves, no people anywhere.
0–2 s: complete stillness, the white LED line reflected on hood, front left fender and driver door, broken and wobbling exactly at the dent in the fender.
2–7 s: the LED light bar slowly glides forward along its cables from above the rear of the car toward the front, and the bright reflected light line slides smoothly across the paint of the door, the fender and the hood; every time the line passes the dent in the front left fender and the crease in the door it bends, splits and wobbles, making the damage clearly visible, on the smooth panels it stays perfectly straight.
2–10 s: the camera very slowly pushes in toward the front left fender in one smooth movement, the dent stays in the center of the frame and in sharp focus at all times.
7–10 s: the light bar comes to rest above the front of the car, the reflection lies across the dent, the camera ends close on the dented fender.
Style: photorealistic, cinematic low-key studio lighting, deep blacks, realistic car paint and metal reflections, ultra sharp, stable.
Avoid: cuts, scene change, people, hands, text, logos, badges, changing car shape, morphing, extra cars, flicker.

## V2 Messen (Startbild 02-messen, MiniMax H3 Unlimited, 768P, 16:9, 10 s)

One single continuous 10-second shot, real-time, no cuts. Use the reference image as the exact first frame. Layout, left to right: front of the dark graphite unbranded hatchback with the dented front left fender, the kneeling damage assessor in a black polo shirt on the right, holding a compact black digital paint thickness gauge in his right hand. His left forearm rests on his knee and stays there the whole time. The assessor stays in frame the whole time, his right hand never lets go of the gauge.
0–2 s: he holds the round sensor tip of the gauge flat on the fender just above the dent and looks at the display.
2–3 s: he lifts the gauge one centimeter off the paint and moves it slowly ten centimeters down to the deepest point of the dent.
3–5 s: he presses the sensor tip flat onto the dent and holds still, reading the display, his eyebrows slightly raised.
5–6 s: he lifts the gauge again and moves it to the right onto the front edge of the driver door next to the crease.
6–8 s: he presses it flat onto the door and holds still, reading.
8–10 s: he lifts the gauge slightly, turns his head and looks along the gap between fender and door.
Camera: one slow smooth arc from left to right of about ten degrees, locked on the gauge tip, focus always on the gauge and the dent.
Style: photorealistic, cinematic low-key studio light, realistic hands and skin, ultra sharp.
Avoid: cuts, text or readable numbers on the display, logos, extra people, hands leaving the frame, morphing face or hands, the gauge changing shape, flicker.

## V3 Karriere (Startbild 08-karriere, Wan 3.0 Unlimited, 480P, 16:9, 10 s)

One single continuous 10-second shot, real-time, no cuts. Use the reference image as the exact first frame. Layout: the young woman in the black softshell workwear jacket with cyan details stands in the right third of the frame holding a tablet in both hands, a dark graphite unbranded hatchback behind her on the left, modern assessment hall with LED strip lights. She stays in frame the whole time, both hands stay on the tablet.
0–3 s: she looks into the camera with a calm, confident slight smile, breathing naturally.
3–5 s: she lowers her eyes to the tablet and taps the screen twice with her right index finger.
5–7 s: she turns her head and shoulders slightly to the left and looks at the car behind her.
7–10 s: she turns back toward the camera and gives a short, self-assured nod with a slight smile.
Camera: one slow smooth push-in from a medium shot to a medium close-up, focus always on her face.
Style: photorealistic, cinematic, modern, realistic skin, natural movement.
Avoid: cuts, text, logos, extra people, morphing face or hands, tablet changing shape, flicker.
